# AI Learning Resources

A community-curated collection of freely accessible AI learning resources maintained by Bharat AI Mission.

Our goal is to help students, developers, educators, and researchers learn AI through trusted learning materials and practical projects.

## How to Use This Collection

Resources are organized by difficulty level:

- **Beginner:** No or minimal prior AI knowledge required.
- **Intermediate:** Basic programming or AI knowledge recommended.
- **Advanced:** Deeper technical knowledge or specialized experience recommended.

## Contribution Guidelines

1. Recommend resources that are freely accessible.
2. Verify that links work before submitting changes.
3. Include a brief, factual description of each resource.
4. Select the appropriate difficulty level.
5. Prefer reputable educational institutions, official documentation, research organizations, and practical learning projects.

## Contributing

Want to add a resource? Open an issue in the [AI Builders Lab repository](https://github.com/bharataimission/ai-builders-lab/issues) or submit a pull request.

Every contribution helps make AI education more accessible.
