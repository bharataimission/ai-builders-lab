# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Bharat AI Mission is committed to building an open, inclusive, and welcoming community that democratizes AI through open-source collaboration.

We want every participant to feel respected, regardless of their experience level, background, language, identity, or location.

## Expected Behaviour

Community members are expected to:

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome beginners and help them learn.
- Give feedback focused on ideas and work, not individuals.
- Respect different perspectives, languages, and experiences.
- Share knowledge openly and acknowledge others' contributions.
- Protect personal information and respect intellectual property.
- Keep discussions relevant, safe, and professional.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insults, or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sharing malicious content, credentials, or confidential information.
- Any other conduct that makes participation unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the Bharat AI Mission maintainers privately through an official community contact channel.

Please include relevant context or links where possible. Reports will be handled as sensitively as possible.

Do not publish private or sensitive details in public GitHub issues.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to Bharat AI Mission repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces when participants are representing or interacting with the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Our Commitment

We aim to foster a community where people can learn, experiment, collaborate, and build open-source AI solutions for India's diverse needs.

**Bharat AI Mission — Democratizing AI for a Billion Dreams.**
